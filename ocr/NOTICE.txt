OCR dependencies, loaded only when screenshot recognition is requested:

Tesseract.js 7.0.0 (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js
Tesseract.js-core 7.0.0 (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core
Japanese traineddata downloaded by Tesseract.js createWorker('jpn').
Tesseract language data (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata

Recognition runs locally. No screenshot upload or external runtime download is used.
Keep the ocr directory and screenshot-ocr.js alongside index.html when deploying.
